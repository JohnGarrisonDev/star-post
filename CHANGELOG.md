# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-24)
A fairness pass on the campaign. The systems now get harder steadily from sector to sector, and no system needs a pixel-perfect launch any more.
- The tightest systems are kinder: Special Delivery, Scrapyard, Bulk Mail, Night Shift and Last Post have more room to post every letter, and Mind the Gravel, Tailwind and In One Ear are easier to dock in.
- Rebound and Push and Pull now lean on their planet's pull, and Bank Shot needs both the moon and the planet.
- Bulk Mail and the last three systems show a little more of the aim line.
- Same systems, same names, same order: your stars and unlocks are kept. Twenty systems were retuned; on those the old best time no longer applies, so it is cleared (your stars stay), and par has been set again for the new layout (some pars are now a little quicker, some slower).
- The Daily delivery is unchanged.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-24)
- Fixed a drawing error that could spam the console when the game area was hidden or collapsed.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)
The orbital post office opens.
- One launch, then gravity: pull back, let go, and curve round planets through mailbox rings to the dock.
- 30 systems in three sectors: Home Orbit, The Bouncy Belt (rubber moons, boost gates, puffs) and The Odd Deep (moving planets, wormholes, repulsor stars).
- Stars for docking, for posting every letter, and for doing it inside par. Best times saved. You can always skip one system.
- Daily delivery: a new system every day, the same for everyone. Fewest launches wins, with streaks and a result you can copy.
- A dotted aim preview, the ghosts of your last three attempts, instant retry and hold-to-fast-forward.
- Mouse, touch and keyboard, with nudge buttons for fine aiming. Turns sideways to fill a portrait phone.
