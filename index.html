Star Post

One launch. Gravity does the rest.

How to play

  • You get one launch. After that gravity steers: planets pull, and slower launches bend more.
  • Fly through every mailbox ring to post its letter, then reach the Post Office dock.
  • Docking earns one star, posting every letter two, doing it inside the par time three.
  • Your last trails stay on screen. Change the angle or power a little and go again.
  • Rubber moons bounce you, gates boost you, wormholes move you, repulsor stars shove you away. Rocks just bonk.
  • The Daily delivery is one new system a day, the same for everyone: post it all in the fewest launches.

Controls

  • Mouse or touch: press anywhere, pull back like a slingshot, let go to launch. Longer pull, more power. Changed your mind? Slide back to where you pressed and let go, or touch the screen with a second finger.
  • Fine tuning: the arrow and plus/minus buttons under the game nudge the aim, then press Launch.
  • Keyboard: ← → aim, ↑ ↓ power (hold Shift for fine steps), Space to launch.
  • Puffs (later systems): tap or press Space in flight for a push straight ahead. Swipe, or use the arrow keys, to push another way. Holding a finger down slows time while you choose.
  • In flight, the Retry and Fast buttons (or R and hold F) retry at once and fast-forward. Esc or the menu button opens the menu.
  • Phone: tap Play full screen under the game. It works upright or sideways.
